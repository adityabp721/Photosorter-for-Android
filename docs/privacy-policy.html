PhotoSorter Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · App ID: com.adityabalagere.photosorter

PhotoSorter organizes the photos on your Android phone into folders and helps you remove duplicates. By default, all of this happens on your phone. The developer runs no servers and does not receive, store or sell any of your data.

What the app accesses on your phone

  • Your photos (Android "Photos and videos" permission), to analyze, copy, move and find duplicates.
  • Photo locations stored inside your photos (GPS, "photo location" permission), to recognize trips.
  • Notifications, to show progress and results of long-running jobs.

Copies are saved in Pictures/PhotoSorter/ on your phone. Photos you choose to remove go to Android's trash, where they stay for 30 days before Android deletes them.

Data sent to Google is handled under Google's terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API and Google services. It travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. PhotoSorter does not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Your API key

If you use Cloud mode, your Gemini API key is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and is sent only to Google with your requests.

Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything the app stored on your phone, uninstall PhotoSorter. Copies it made in Pictures/PhotoSorter/ remain until you delete them.

Children

PhotoSorter is not directed at children under 13.
